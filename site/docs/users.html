Users and spaces

Accounts, groups and spaces are managed by administrators in the Control panel.

Users

Open Control panel › Users.

  • Create an account with a username and a first password. Ask the person to change it after signing in.
  • Role: administrators can open the Control panel. Users cannot.
  • Permissions: whether the person may edit, delete and share. Without them the account can only view.
  • Space size: how much the person may store. 0 means no limit.
  • Disable an account to stop it signing in without deleting its files. Its app passwords stop working too.
  • Reset two-factor sign-in is for someone who lost their phone and their recovery codes. A shield next to the name shows who has it on.
  • Devices lists the browsers where the person is signed in. Sign out one of them, or all of them with Sign out everywhere. It is recorded in the activity log and in the person's sign-in history.

The Used column includes items in the trash.

Sign-in security

Under Control panel › General › Users:

  • Require two-factor sign-in for password accounts: signing in with a password also asks for a code from an authenticator app. People who haven't set it up are asked to right after their password, before they get in, and can't turn it off. Devices that are already signed in stay signed in; sign them out under Devices if needed. Accounts that sign in with Microsoft, Google or GitHub rely on that provider, and app passwords keep working without a code.
  • Minimum password length: 6 characters unless you choose more, up to 64. It applies to passwords set or changed from then on.

How people set up two-factor sign-in is described in Your account.

Groups

A group is a list of people, for example one department. Give a group access to a space or folder instead of adding people one by one. When someone joins or leaves the group, their access follows.

Spaces

Open Control panel › Spaces to create team spaces, set their size limit and choose where their files are stored.

  • Add members from the space's Members dialog and give each a role.
  • To let everyone create team spaces themselves, switch it on under Control panel › General. A space created this way gets the same size limit as its creator, and each person can create up to 20.

What administrators can see

Administrators manage every team space and the company space, including their trash. They cannot open personal spaces.

Forgotten passwords

An administrator sets a new password under Control panel › Users. If the administrator's own password is lost, see Troubleshooting.
