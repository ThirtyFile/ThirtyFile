Sharing

There are two ways to share: with people who have an account, and with anyone through a link.

Share with colleagues

Right-click a folder or file and choose Share with…. Type a username or group, choose what they may do, and optionally a date when the access ends, then click Add.

The item appears under Shared with me in their menu.

Roles

RoleCan
ViewerBrowse, preview and download
EditorAlso upload and edit; delete and create share links if their account is allowed to (Control panel › Users)
ManagerAlso manage members and sharing
OwnerAlso delete the space

Share with a link

Right-click an item and choose Create share link, or select it and click Create share link on the toolbar. Anyone with the link can view and download, without an account.

  • Expiration: the link stops working after 1, 7, 30 or 90 days, or never.
  • Password: visitors must enter it before they see anything. Send the password separately from the link.
  • Download limit: the link stops working after this many downloads.

To change a link, delete it and create a new one.

Your links

My share links in the menu lists every link you created, with its expiry date and how often it was used. Delete a link there to stop it working right away.

A link also stops working when you lose access to the item yourself, for example when you leave a team space.

Spaces

A space is a drive with its own members and size limit.

  • My files: your personal space. Only you can see it, unless you share something from it.
  • All files: the company-wide space. An administrator can give it another name.
  • Team spaces: for a department or project. Managers add and remove members.
